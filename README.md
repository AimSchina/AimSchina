# Selected Research Projects

## 🧬 T- & B-cell receptor diversity: shaping immune diversity and potential biomarkers for immunotherapy

The role of T- and B-cell receptor diversity in shaping immune response and association with other critical established or emerging biomarkers of the tumor microenvironment. Additional investigation of the potential of T- and B-cell receptor diversity as single biomarkers or combined with other biomarkers in comprehensive models for predicting response to immune checkpoint inhibitors.

[View project →](https://github.com/TILccit/TCR_BCR_antiPD1)

## 📊 FDG-PET scans and early treatment discontinuation

Determining whether regular FDG-PET scans can be valuable in identifying real-world patients with advances melanoma who might benefit from early treatment discontinuation.

[View project →](https://github.com/TILccit/FDG_PET_antiPD1_discontinuation)

## 🧬 Real-world anti-PD-1 treatment in metastatic melanoma

Exploring the effect of first-line anti-PD-1-based treatments on real-world patients with metastatic melanoma and development of a 4-year prognostic prediction model for overall survival.

[View project →](https://github.com/TILccit/RealMelanoma_antiPD1_based)
